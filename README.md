# Logixa Launchpad

Build a modern, professional homepage for "LOGIXA", a global logistics and freight forwarding company. 

Design direction: clean, corporate, trustworthy — inspired by enterprise logistics sites like Scan Global Logistics (scangl.com), but simpler and more startup-modern. Use a bold, confident visual style with lots of white space, large hero imagery, and smooth scroll-triggered animations (fade-up, subtle parallax on hero).

BRAND

- Name: LOGIXA

- Tagline: "Uncomplicating Global Logistics, Every Mile."

- Logo: a globe icon with an upward arrow/compass mark (orange + navy), text "LOGIXA" in bold navy, with a thin orange underline accent

- Color palette: Deep Navy Blue (#0B2A4A) as primary, Vivid Orange (#F5821F) as accent, White/Light Gray (#F7F8FA) backgrounds, Charcoal (#2B2B2B) body text

- Typography: modern sans-serif (e.g., "Inter" or "Poppins") — bold, large headings; clean body text

SECTIONS (top to bottom):

1. STICKY NAVBAR

   - Logo left, nav links center/right: Services, Industries, About, Track Shipment, Contact

   - CTA button "Get a Quote" (orange, rounded)

   - Transparent on hero, solid navy/white on scroll

2. HERO SECTION

   - Full-width background: video-style or high-quality image collage of cargo ship, plane, and truck (use a dark navy gradient overlay for text contrast)

   - Big headline: "Your Global Partner in Freight & Supply Chain Solutions"

   - Subheadline: "Air, Sea, Land & Project Cargo — delivered with commitment, collaboration, and customer intimacy."

   - Two CTAs: "Get a Free Quote" (orange filled button) + "Track Your Shipment" (outline button)

   - A floating "Track & Trace" search widget card (input for tracking number + dropdown for AWB/BL/Container No., like a mini tool) overlapping the bottom of the hero

3. TRUST BAR

   - A thin strip below hero with 4-5 stats in a row: "10+ Years Experience", "50+ Global Partners", "100+ Ports Covered", "24/7 Support" — icons + bold numbers

4. ABOUT / MISSION-VISION SECTION

   - Two-column layout: left = image (cargo ship/globe illustration), right = text

   - Heading: "Who We Are"

   - Short paragraph based on: "Working towards Customer-centric solutions by providing quality supply chain services. We deliver top-quality logistics at cost-effective prices — consistent value and dependability for customers large and small."

   - Below it, 3 small cards for "Our Vision — The 3 C's": Commitment, Collaboration, Customer Intimacy (icon + short text each)

5. SERVICES SECTION (core section)

   - Heading: "Our Services" with subtext "End-to-end logistics solutions tailored to your business"

   - Responsive grid of 8 service cards with icons, hover-lift animation:

     1. Air Freight

     2. Sea Freight (LCL & FCL)

     3. Land Freight

     4. Custom Clearance

     5. Chartering & Break Bulk

     6. Special Projects Cargo

     7. Bulk Liquid Solutions (ISO Tanks & Flexi Tanks)

     8. Storage & Warehousing

   - Each card: icon, title, 1-line description, "Learn More" link (orange text with arrow)

6. WHY CHOOSE US SECTION

   - Dark navy background, white text, contrast section

   - 4-column icon grid: "Global Network", "On-Time Delivery", "Custom Clearance Experts", "24/7 Cargo Tracking"

7. HOW IT WORKS (process strip)

   - Horizontal 4-step timeline with connecting dotted line and icons:

     Step 1: Request a Quote → Step 2: Plan & Book → Step 3: Ship & Track → Step 4: Deliver & Confirm

8. INDUSTRIES / CARGO TYPES SECTION (optional visual strip)

   - Icon row showing cargo handling capabilities: Fragile Goods, Hazardous/DG Cargo, Heavy Equipment, Household Goods, Reefer Containers, Personal Effects

9. TESTIMONIALS / PARTNERS SECTION

   - Light gray background

   - Heading: "Trusted by Businesses Worldwide"

   - Logo carousel placeholder row (grayscale logos) + 2-3 testimonial cards with quote, name, designation

10. CALL-TO-ACTION BANNER

    - Full-width orange or navy gradient banner

    - Heading: "Ready to Simplify Your Supply Chain?"

    - CTA button: "Contact Our Team"

11. FOOTER

    - Dark navy background, white/gray text

    - 4 columns: 

       Col 1: Logo + address "Office No. 306, Sai Chamber, Sec 11, CBD Belapur, Navi Mumbai - 400614" + social icons

       Col 2: Services (list all 8 services as links)

       Col 3: Company (About, Careers, Contact, Track Shipment)

       Col 4: Newsletter signup (email input + subscribe button)

    - Bottom bar: "© 2026 LOGIXA Logistics & Consultants. All rights reserved." + Privacy Policy / Terms links

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

- Fully responsive (mobile-first)

- Smooth scroll animations (AOS-style fade/slide on scroll)

- Hover states on all buttons/cards

- Use placeholder logistics imagery/icons (trucks, ships, planes, containers, warehouse)

- Component-based structure (Navbar, Hero, TrustBar, About, Services, WhyChooseUs, Process, Industries, Testimonials, CTA, Footer)
